## How the data was produced

Everything was **encoded by hand** into one typed file per track (`tracks/*.ts`). HPAO (and ANSC, for pre-vet, and Letters & Sciences, for pre-law) publish categories, not UMD course codes, so each category was mapped to UMD courses using UMD's own pages above (the HPAO nursing and AP pages, which do name UMD courses; the Biological Sciences catalog page; the NEUR pre-med plan; the ANSC guide; the pre-law timeline document and slides; and the Spring 2027 Schedule of Classes). Every mapping choice is in the track's `reviewNotes`, and every track is `verified: false` until the owner signs off.

**The course-existence fixture** (`test/fixtures/umd-courses.json`) is built from `packages/course-data/.cache/soc-202701.json` in the main checkout (the Spring 2027 Schedule of Classes), the only Schedule of Classes snapshot cached anywhere in this repo — no fall term is cached. A course not being offered that one term doesn't make its number invalid (a student who already took it would otherwise see the requirement as unfairly unmet), so requirement alternatives the Spring 2027 schedule doesn't confirm are still encoded and still in the fixture, each with its own note in `tracks/common.ts`'s `MAPPING_NOTES` and in the fixture: BSCI161, BSCI171, BSCI330, PHYS141 and PHYS142 (each also named directly on the Biological Sciences catalog page, a stronger source than the schedule alone), and CHEM146, CHEM177 and CHEM247 (also CHEM237, which is in the Spring 2027 schedule on its own but pairs with CHEM247 here) -- confirmed by UMD's Chemistry major catalog page (main session, 2026-09-27; see `MAPPING_NOTES.genChem`/`.organic`). PHIL170 (Introduction to Logic), used in pre-law's suggested courses, is the same idea: not in the Spring 2027 schedule (likely a fall-only course), but added to the fixture because the pre-law timeline document above names it directly by course number.

**The three newest tracks** (Anesthesiologist Assistant, Dental Hygiene, Genetic Counseling) were added the same way, from their own HPAO "Explore Careers" pages (fetched 2026-09-26). Two new fixture entries were needed: BSCI410 (Molecular Genetics) and BSCI416 (Human Genetics), for Genetic Counseling's "Advanced Genetics" category; both are confirmed directly in the Spring 2027 Schedule of Classes, so neither needs a fixture note. Every other course these three tracks name (general/organic chemistry, biochemistry, biology, physics, calculus, statistics, English, psychology, anatomy & physiology, college algebra) was already in the fixture from an earlier track.

**Pre-law's suggested courses** come from the "Pre-Law Courses" Google Slides embedded in the pre-law page. The slides render each course as vector artwork rather than text, but the accessibility label on each slide (read from the raw fetched HTML) gives its course numbers and titles as plain text; that's how the list in `tracks/pre-law.ts` was extracted. Only the slide's entries confirmed against the Spring 2027 Schedule of Classes are kept (plus PHIL170, above); the rest (most of GVPT, HIST, PHIL, FMSC, CCJS, BMGT and CLAS, and everything under American Studies) aren't offered that term and are left out, noted in `tracks/pre-law.ts`'s `reviewNotes`. The "bold entries are strongly recommended" distinction mentioned in the row above is genuinely lost (the accessibility label carries no formatting), not the course numbers themselves.
