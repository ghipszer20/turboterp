# College intro courses (researched 2026-09-29)

Owner ruling (rulings.md, "Owner notes 2026-09-29"): each college's first-semester intro course is a college requirement in the audit for students who enter as freshmen, and sits in first-fall of every sample plan and the default plan.

Sources: the Academic Catalog's approved-course pages (`academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/<prefix>/`), the Orientation office's UNIV100 page (`orientation.umd.edu/transitionprograms/univ100`) and ARHU's advising page.

| College | Course | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| CMNS | CMNS100 Becoming a Science Terp (1 cr) | Required (owner) | Catalog: "must be first semester CMNS freshman student. Credit Only Granted for: UNIV100 or CMNS100." Accept UNIV100 as the alternative. |
| ARHU | ARHU158 Explorations in Arts and Humanities (3 cr) | Required | ARHU: "All ARHU first-year students (excluding students participating in certain special programs) must take ARHU158 ... during their first semester on campus." Catalog: "Must be in a major in ARHU; and freshman standing." |
| SPHL | UNIV100 The Student in the University (1 cr) | Required | Orientation: "All new students in the School of Public Health (SPHL) ... are required to enroll in UNIV100." |
| INFO | INST101 Bits and Bytes of Computer and Information Sciences (1 cr; = CMSC100) | Recommended (not confirmed as required) | Catalog: "For first time freshmen and first time transfer students." |
| BSOS, EDUC, ENGR, ARCH, AGNR, Letters and Sciences | UNIV100 (1 cr) | Strongly encouraged, not required | Orientation: "strongly encouraged to enroll in UNIV100." |
| BMGT, JOUR, PLCY | none found | — | Their first-year courses (BMGT110, JOUR181/JOUR200, PLCY100/101) are major requirements, already encoded in the programs. |

Encoding decision (main session, 2026-09-29): **Required** rows become an audit requirement for freshman entrants; **recommended / strongly encouraged** rows only go into first-fall of sample and default plans (no audit requirement). Colleges with none found get nothing. Flag for the owner: whether INST101 is required for INFO, and whether "strongly encouraged" UNIV100 should be required.
