# New pre-professional tracks: research (2026-09-29)

Scope: owner ruling "add every pre-professional track that has actual fixed prerequisites (set by a national accreditor, licensing board or professional body)". Already encoded and skipped here: medicine, dentistry, PA, vet, pharmacy, optometry, podiatry, PT, OT, nursing, law, AA, dental hygiene, genetic counseling.

**Evidence caveat.** Pages were read through a fetch tool that summarizes, and several official pages 404'd, returned 403, or were PDFs it could not parse. Items marked (S) come from a search-result summary of the official site, not a page read in full: builders must re-verify them against the quoted URL before setting `verified: true`. Nothing here is signed off by the owner.

UMD course codes were checked against `academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/<prefix>/` on 2026-09-29 (they exist; titles given where fetched).

## 1. Speech-language pathology (ASHA) - ENCODE

- Body: ASHA, 2020 SLP Certification Standards. https://www.asha.org/certification/2020-slp-certification-standards/
- Degree: "a master's, doctoral, or other recognized post-baccalaureate degree" (Standard I). Graduate program needs "a minimum of 36 semester credit hours at the graduate level".
- Standard IV-A prerequisite areas (no credit minimums stated; each must be its own coursework):
  - Biological sciences: "human or animal sciences (e.g., biology, human anatomy and physiology, neuroanatomy and neurophysiology)".
  - Chemistry OR physics.
  - Social/behavioral sciences: "analysis and investigation of human and animal behavior".
  - Statistics: research methodology "cannot be used to fulfill this requirement".
- UMD courses: biology BSCI170 + BSCI171/BSCI180, or BSCI160/161; chemistry CHEM131 (+132); physics PHYS121/122 or PHYS131/132; social/behavioral PSYC100, SOCY100; statistics STAT100 (Elementary Statistics and Probability) or STAT400. Communication-sciences background (not required by ASHA, expected by most programs): HESP202 (Intro to Hearing and Speech Sciences), HESP305/306 (Anatomy and Physiology of Speech), HESP307, HESP313 (Neuroscience for Speech and Hearing), HESP400, LING200.
- Exam/GPA: none stated by ASHA; programs set GRE and GPA. Application via CSDCAS (program-level; check).
- Verdict: ENCODE. Categories are fixed by ASHA without credit minimums (encode as one course each). UMD's HESP major makes this the most likely used new track.

## 2. Audiology (ASHA / CAA) - SKIP

- https://www.asha.org/certification/2020-audiology-certification-standards/ : applicants "must hold a doctoral degree in audiology from a program accredited by the CAA". The standards page states no prerequisite coursework (points to an "AuD Coursework Outline"); admission prerequisites are set per AuD program.
- Verdict: SKIP (no body-fixed list). Optional alias of the SLP categories if the owner wants it; that would be a judgment call, not sourced.

## 3. Chiropractic (CCE) - ENCODE (S)

- Body: Council on Chiropractic Education (cce-usa.org). The standards PDF URL I tried 404'd; the home page links /guidance.html and /publications.html. Builder must find the current "Accreditation Standards" PDF there.
- Reported (S, search summary): 90 semester hours of undergraduate study; "a minimum of 24 semester hours in life and physical science courses with at least half of these courses having a substantive laboratory component". GPA: results conflict (3.0 standard with a 2.7-2.75 alternative admission track; older wording 2.75). Verify the current number.
- UMD courses: life science BSCI160/161 or BSCI170/171, BSCI201/202 or BSCI223; physical science CHEM131/132, CHEM231/232, PHYS121/122, PHYS131/132.
- Exam: none for admission (NBCE boards come after the DC).
- Verdict: ENCODE. One main category (24 cr life/physical science, half with lab) plus a 90-credit milestone and GPA note.

## 4. CPA licensure in Maryland - ENCODE (different shape)

- Body: Maryland State Board of Public Accountancy (Maryland Dept of Labor). https://labor.maryland.gov/license/cpa/cpaexam/cpaexameducreq.shtml
- To sit for the exam: "120 semester hours or 180 undergraduate quarter hours or the equivalent", including 30 semester hours in accounting and ethics (27 accounting + 3 ethics).
- For licensure: "150 semester credit hours or 225 quarter credit hours of education", plus Group II business requirements.
- Accounting: minimum 30 semester hours, with coursework in auditing, accounting information systems, federal income tax, ethics, financial accounting, electives.
- Business: at least 18 semester hours across five of nine subject areas (statistics, economics, finance, management, marketing, data analytics, communication, IT/systems, quantitative methods), plus 3 hours business law.
- Dates: current requirements apply now; prior standards usable until June 30, 2026. The fetch summary is not verbatim, so the builder must re-read the page for exact grouping wording before encoding.
- UMD courses (titles fetched): BMGT220, BMGT221 (Principles of Accounting I, II), BMGT310, BMGT311 (Intermediate Accounting I, II), BMGT321 Managerial Accounting, BMGT323 Taxation of Individuals, BMGT326 Accounting Systems, BMGT422 Auditing Theory and Practice, BMGT411 Ethics and Professionalism in Accounting, BMGT417 Taxation of Corporations, BMGT380 Business Law I. Business breadth: STAT100/STAT400, ECON200/ECON201, BMGT340 Business Finance.
- Exam/GPA: Uniform CPA Exam after education; no GPA from the Board.
- Verdict: ENCODE. A credit-count track rather than a course-prerequisite track: needs categories with minimum credits and a 150-credit total milestone. Most UMD accounting majors already meet most of it; the value is the gap check.

## 6. Orthotics and prosthetics (NCOPE) - SKIP

- Verdict: SKIP.

## 7. Perfusion (AC-PE / CAAHEP) - SKIP

- Bachelor's degree required (S). Typical prerequisites (8 cr biology, 8 cr chemistry, 6-8 cr A&P, 3.0) came from single-school pages (NKU, QU, VCU), not AC-PE. Small field, varied.
- Verdict: SKIP (no body-set list).

## 8. Art therapy (AATA / ACATE) - ENCODE, low priority (S)

- Body: AATA, https://arttherapy.org/becoming-art-therapist/ : "A master's degree is necessary for entry-level practice." Foundational studio art ("drawing, painting, digital art, clay or sculpture") and at least "a prerequisite course in ... developmental psychology and also abnormal psychology". Standards are set by ACATE/CAAHEP; programs add their own.
- Numbers (S, from program sources, not AATA's page): about 18 semester hours studio art (drawing, painting, 3-D) and about 12 hours psychology. Verify against ACATE's Educational Standards.
- UMD courses: ARTT100, ARTT110 (Elements of Drawing I), ARTT200 (Three-Dimensional Art Fundamentals), ARTT210, ARTT255, ARTT320 (Elements of Painting); psychology PSYC100, PSYC353, PSYC330, PSYC355.
- Verdict: ENCODE only if the owner wants it; credit numbers are program norms ("so standard nearly every program requires them"), not AATA text.

## 9. Naturopathic medicine (CNME / AANMC) - ENCODE, low priority (S)

- Bodies: CNME (accreditor) https://cnme.org/ ; AANMC https://aanmc.org/academic-prerequisites/ (403 to my fetcher; CNME handbook PDF unparseable). Content from search summary.
- Reported (S): baccalaureate degree; 24 credit hours in life and physical sciences, lecture plus lab; all courses C or higher; GPA 3.00 (alternative track 2.75-2.99). NPLEX licensing exam after the ND.
- UMD courses: BSCI170/171, BSCI180, CHEM131/132, CHEM231/232, BCHM461; PSYC100 (verify whether required).
- Verdict: ENCODE, low priority. Verify against AANMC by hand.

## 10. Medical laboratory science (NAACLS / ASCP BOC) - ENCODE (S)

- Bodies: NAACLS https://naacls.org/ ; ASCP Board of Certification https://www.ascp.org/boc/explore-credentials/view-all-credentials/MLS (403 to my fetcher). NAACLS 2024 Standards PDF unparseable.
- Reported (S): baccalaureate with 16 semester hours biology (including 1 semester microbiology) and 16 semester hours chemistry (including 1 semester organic chemistry or biochemistry), plus a NAACLS-accredited MLS program. NAACLS standards themselves only require "appropriate prerequisite coursework", so the 16/16 is the ASCP eligibility route.
- UMD courses: biology BSCI170/171, BSCI180, BSCI223; chemistry CHEM131/132, CHEM231/232, BCHM461.
- Verdict: ENCODE. The 16 + 16 is common to nearly every post-bacc MLS program.

## 11. Dietetics (ACEND DPD) - SKIP

- Since 2024, CDR requires a minimum of a master's degree to sit for the RD exam (eatrightpro.org, cdrnet.org). ACEND standards are competency-based, with no fixed pre-degree course list; UMD's NFSC dietetics major already serves this.
- Verdict: SKIP.

## 12. Medical physics (CAMPEP) - ENCODE, minor

- Body: CAMPEP graduate standards, https://campep.org/GraduateStandards.pdf . Entering students need "a strong foundation in basic physics, documented by either a degree in physics or a degree in engineering or other area of physical science with physics education equivalent to a minor in physics (including at least three upper level undergraduate physics courses or equivalent required for a physics major)"; remedial education allowed. No credit minimums or GPA; programs set GRE/GPA.
- UMD courses: PHYS161 / PHYS260/261 (or PHYS171/172), upper-level PHYS401, PHYS404, PHYS411, PHYS420; math MATH140/141/241/246.
- Verdict: ENCODE as a single-category track (physics-minor equivalent plus 3 upper-level PHYS). Low priority.

## 13. Actuarial (SOA VEE) - ENCODE (S)

- Body: Society of Actuaries, https://www.soa.org/education/exam-req/edu-VEE/ . Three VEE topics: "Economics", "Accounting and Finance", "Mathematical Statistics". Candidates must "pass two SOA, CAS, or CIA actuarial exams before applying for VEE credit". The SOA keeps a directory of pre-approved courses per subject; grade minimums are in that directory and were not visible to me.
- UMD courses (confirm in the SOA approved-course directory): Economics ECON200 + ECON201; Accounting and Finance BMGT220 + BMGT340; Mathematical Statistics STAT400 + STAT401 (or STAT410).
- Exam: Exams P and FM come first; no GPA.
- Verdict: ENCODE after the builder confirms UMD's courses in the SOA directory. It is credit-by-course, not admission.
