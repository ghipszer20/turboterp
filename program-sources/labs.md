## PSYC401: Biological Bases of Behavior Laboratory (4 credits)

- Source: UMD catalog, https://academiccatalog.umd.edu/undergraduate/approved-courses/psyc/

- Restriction: Permission of instructor; and must be in Psychology program; and must have earned a minimum of 85 credits.
- Credit only granted for: PSYC401, NEUR405, BSCI455 or BSCI454.
- Description: Students will study some of the key concepts in neuroscience by combining behavioral experiments with electrode recordings from neurons controlling the behaviors.
