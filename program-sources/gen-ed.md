## FSMA and FSAR
Summary Chart (under both Math FSMA and Analytic Reasoning FSAR):
> Courses that are designated as both FSMA and FSAR will satisfy both requirements.

Summary Chart, Math: "AP/IB exemptions are allowed."
The AP chart lists exam credit such as "MATH 140 (FSMA and FSAR)" and "STAT 100 (FSMA and FSAR)".

## One Distributive Studies category per course
Summary Chart, Distributive Studies (25 credits/8 courses):
> While a course may be approved for more than one Distributive Studies category, a student may not use one course
> to meet more than one category requirement. The student may select the preferred category.

FAQ:
> When a course has two Distributive Studies designations, why can't I fulfill two requirements with it? … Because
> students need a minimum of 8 DS courses, students are unable to use one course to meet two DS requirements.

The catalog page doesn't state this rule; cite the Summary Chart or the FAQ.

## Big Question (SCIS) is within the 8 DS courses
Summary Chart:
> • 2 courses must be Big Question courses.

> BIG QUESTION (FORMERLY I-SERIES) 6 credits/2 courses • Big Question courses must also fulfill a Distributive
> Studies category.

> SCIS • All students must complete two Distribute Studies courses that are approved in the Big Question course
> category. 6 credits*

"*indicates that units may be double counted." So a Big Question course counts as SCIS **and** as the one DS category
it fills. It must actually be one of the student's 8 DS courses. Example: PHYS235 (Testudo: DSHS, DSNS, SCIS) fills
one of DSHS or DSNS, plus Big Question.

## AP / IB limits
Summary Chart, Distributive Studies:
> • Only 6 courses can be from AP or IB credit.

DS page:
> AP credit for Distributive Studies is limited to six of the eight courses.

> Two of the eight courses must be I-Series courses. AP credit may not be used to satisfy the I-Series requirement.

Reading (session B, 2026-10-06): the 6-of-8 limit covers AP and IB credit (the Summary Chart says both). The DS page
names only AP for the Big Question bar. The sources are silent on IB here, so IB is treated the same: that reading
can't mark Big Question met wrongly (owner rule, PROJECT_MEMORY section 2). Neither chart awards SCIS anyway.

## Diversity
Summary Chart:
> • 2 Understanding Plural Societies courses or 1 Understanding Plural Societies course AND 1 Cultural Competence
> course are required. • Diversity courses may also fulfill a Distributive Studies category.

DS page: "A Diversity requirement may be fulfilled by a course that is approved for both a Diversity category and for
a Distributive Studies category."
Catalog: "Courses fulfilling the Diversity requirement may double-count in an approved Distributive Studies category."

## Credit minimums (Summary Chart, re-read 2026-10-07)
Text extracted with pdfjs from the Summary Chart (revised April 2, 2024):
> FUNDAMENTAL STUDIES 15 credits/5 courses … Academic Writing FSAW … 3 credits; Professional Writing FSPW 3 credits;
> Oral Communication FSOC 3 credits; Math FSMA … 3 credits; Analytic Reasoning FSAR … 3 credits

> DISTRIBUTIVE STUDIES 25 credits/8 courses • 2 courses must be from each area. … Natural Sciences DSNS/DSNL • At least
> one course must have lab component. 7 credits; History and Social Sciences DSHS 6 credits; Humanities DSHU 6 credits;
> Scholarship in Practice DSSP … 6 credits

> TOTALS: 40-46 credits

Reading (2026-10-07): each area needs both its course count and its credits. The catalog has DSSP courses of 1–2
credits (CPGH200, TLPL102) and of 6 credits (ARCH402, ENGL388M), so neither rule alone can stand in for the other.

## Lab pairs
Testudo's Gen Ed text makes 20 lectures a lab science only together with their lab, e.g. CHEM131 "DSNL (if taken with
CHEM132), DSNS", PHYS260 "DSNL (if taken with PHYS261)", PHYS161 "General Education Natural Sciences Lab (DSNL) Course
only when taken concurrently with PHYS275." The AP chart grants the pair together ("CHEM 131 and CHEM 132 (DSNL)"), and
UMD's degree audit lists BSCI160 + BSCI161 together under DSNL for 4 credits. Reading: DSNL needs the lab in the same
term, and the lecture brings the lecture + lab credits to the 7-credit Natural Sciences minimum.
